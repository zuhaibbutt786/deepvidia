AI companies in Pakistan: what freelancers and startups should look for

Interest in AI companies in Pakistan is rising. Whether you hire or freelancing, focus on clear outcomes instead of vague “AI transformation” claims.

Useful signals include: published or open-source examples, defined data annotation processes, computer vision or chatbot case studies, and willingness to discuss edge cases and evaluation metrics.

Deepvidia is based in Gujranwala and publishes open projects across data annotation-related pipelines, computer vision, RAG chatbots, and automation so that work can be inspected rather than only described.

For freelancers, the same bar applies in reverse: portfolios that show real pipelines (detection models, RAG demos, scrapers, voice tools) outperform generic claims about “machine learning experience.”
