FAQ

For schools, colleges and anyone evaluating Deepvidia.

What is Deepvidia’s main service?

Training students of every age in AI, robotics and IoT — with packages for schools, colleges and academies (online and onsite).

Can our school or college book a program?

Yes. Contact us with campus size, age group and preferred mode (online / onsite). We propose a package including kits and instructor days where needed.

Do packages include hardware?

School and onsite packages can include IoT sensor kits and basic robotics hardware. Online packages focus on software labs with optional kit add-ons.

Where are you based?

Gujranwala, Pakistan. We deliver onsite in reachable cities and online nationwide.
