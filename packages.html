How we think about cost

Package floors reflect approximate kit & sensor pools (boards, ultrasonic, temperature, motors, wiring), instructor and assistant time, and coordination. Schools often prefer shared kits; colleges often prefer pair kits for portfolio projects. Online reduces hardware line items. Always confirm a written quote before purchase orders.
